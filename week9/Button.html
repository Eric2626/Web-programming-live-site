EXERCISE 1

Fix a div-button to a real button

Accessible buttons work with a mouse, keyboard, and screen reader.

Press Tab to move between the buttons. Press Enter or Space to activate one.

Select a button to test it.

What was fixed?

  • Replaced <div onclick> with <button type="button">.
  • Added a visible :focus-visible outline for keyboard users.
  • Added aria-label="Close message" to the icon-only button.
  • Both buttons are automatically keyboard accessible using Tab, Enter, and Space.
