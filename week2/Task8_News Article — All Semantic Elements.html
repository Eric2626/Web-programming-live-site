Dota 2 Remains One of the Most Competitive Games

By Eric · Published September 13, 2026

About Dota 2

Dota 2 is a multiplayer online battle arena game where two teams compete against each other. Each player controls a unique hero with different abilities and roles.

Players must work together to destroy the enemy team's Ancient while protecting their own base. Good communication and teamwork are very important.

In Dota 2, teamwork and strategy can be more important than individual skill.

— Dota 2 Community

Why Dota 2 Is Interesting

Dota 2 has many heroes, items, and strategies. Every match can be different depending on the heroes, players, and decisions made during the game.

Players can choose different roles such as Carry, Support, Mid, and Offlane. Learning each role can make players better at the game.

Dota 2 Facts

  • Two teams compete in each match
  • Each team has five players
  • Players choose from many different heroes
  • The main goal is to destroy the enemy Ancient
  • Teamwork and strategy are very important
